How It Began

A Battersea Institution

Café Parisienne opened in 2018 with the aim to create a little corner of Paris in the heart of London.

Today it's a much-loved neighbourhood café on Lavender Hill — known for indulgent breakfasts served all day, hearty lunches and a team that treats every guest like a regular. Just two minutes from Battersea Arts Centre, it's a favourite with the theatre's staff and actors for a pre-show coffee or bite.
