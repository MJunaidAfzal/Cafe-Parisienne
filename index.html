Welcome

A Little Corner of Paris on Lavender Hill

Café Parisienne opened in 2018 with one aim: to bring a little corner of Paris to the heart of London.

Pull up a chair on the terrace under the blue awning, or settle in by the framed photographs inside. From a quick coffee on the way to work to a full English with friends, every plate is cooked fresh and served with genuine warmth — just two minutes from Battersea Arts Centre.
